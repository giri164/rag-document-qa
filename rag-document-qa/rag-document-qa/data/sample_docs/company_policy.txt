Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week, subject to manager approval.
Full-time remote work requires a written request submitted to HR at least 30
days in advance and is granted only for roles marked "remote-eligible" in the
job description.

Leave Policy

All full-time employees accrue 18 days of paid leave per year, credited at
the start of each quarter. Unused leave can be carried over to the next year
up to a maximum of 10 days. Sick leave is tracked separately and does not
count against the paid leave balance.

Expense Reimbursement

Business expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date using
the internal expense portal. Reimbursements over $500 require pre-approval
from a department head. Travel expenses include airfare, lodging, and meals
up to the daily per-diem limit.
